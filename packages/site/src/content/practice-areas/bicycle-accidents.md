A cyclist hit by a car in Florida is usually hurt worse than a driver
would be in the same crash, and the insurance picture is less obvious:
the cyclist has no vehicle policy in play at the scene, yet coverage —
sometimes the cyclist's own — often still applies.

## Preserve the bicycle

Do not repair or discard the bicycle, helmet, or clothing. They are
physical evidence of impact forces and crash mechanics, and their loss
is difficult to undo.
