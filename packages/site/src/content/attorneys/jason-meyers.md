I am the son of Jeff and Lillian Meyers. My father, Jeff, was a F-15
flight mechanic in the Airforce. My mother stayed home with me and my
brother and sister. My mother attended school at night and obtained a
master's degree in education. She went on to be a teacher as me and my
siblings entered our teenage years.

My parents instilled the values of hard work and education in me. I
attended Florida State University where I studied Philosophy and
Religion. After graduating, I worked as a discrimination investigator
at the Florida Commission on Human Relations for three years. I was
encouraged by the staff attorneys at FCHR to attend law school.

I attended law school at Stetson University in Gulfport, located near
St. Petersburg in the Tampa Bay area. After graduating, I began working
as an attorney in Civil Litigation in the area of personal injury,
insurance disputes, property damage, and breach of contract claims. My
practice consisted mostly of representing large corporations. In March
2024, I left the big firm practice to establish a practice in the
community to assist small business owners and individuals with their
legal needs.

I have tried several cases to verdict. I pride myself on my courtroom
experience because it educates and informs the advice that I give to my
clients. I take the time to educate my clients on the litigation
process and what could happen with the understanding that I am capable
and willing to go to trial to see their case through. This is important
when negotiating a result because the other side knows that you are
represented by an attorney that will take the case to trial and thus is
more likely to give you full value during negotiations and potentially
avoiding the costs of litigation.

When I am not practicing law, I am with my wife and two children or on
the tennis court.
