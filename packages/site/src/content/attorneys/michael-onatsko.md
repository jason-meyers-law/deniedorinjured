I am a first-generation American whose parents came to the United States of America seeking freedom from religious persecution. Growing up, I watched my parents build a new life in the States with me and my four siblings. This instilled a strong work ethic that I carry with me to this day through discipline and perseverance.

I obtained my bachelor's degree in Legal Studies at the University of Central Florida and completed my J.D. at Nova Southeastern University. After graduating law school, I returned to Central Florida and began my legal career in civil litigation, focusing on defense work. At two large insurance defense firms, I had the opportunity to represent a wide range of clients, from individuals involved in motor vehicle accidents to major insurance carriers, trucking companies, and Fortune 500 corporations. These early years provided invaluable courtroom experience and sharpened my ability to think strategically.

Since leaving the defense firms, I continue to focus on civil litigation. I have dedicated my career to representing individuals and businesses across Florida, both plaintiffs and defendants, in a variety of legal disputes.

When I am not in the office or the courtroom, I spend my time outdoors boating with my fiancée and our three dogs.
